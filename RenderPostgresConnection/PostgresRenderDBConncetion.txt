How to connect the Render's Postgres DB

Step 1. - We need to create the DB on the Render with following steps

	-- Go on any browser(Google) and search as render web hosting
	-- Then Login there if you have account. So firstly sing up and then sign in with your credentials
	-- Then you are able to see home interface of the 'Render' 
	-- Then click on the 'New' button
	-- Now you need to click on the "Postgres" option
	-- Then provide your name of the instance and provide the all info. which are mandatory
	-- Select your plan then Render will be provide the there credentials to you. Copy these credentials

Step 2. - Create you own server on pgAdmin with the help of the provide credentials by the Render 
	
	-- Open the pgAdmin (Postgres DB)
	-- After that you can able to see the one option which is "Add New Server", click on that
	-- Then you need to provide the Name in General tab
	-- Next you need go in 'connection' tab and provide the values of the 'Hostname/address (Ex, ************************************************) , Password, Username' which is 	    provided by the "Render"
	-- And end of task click on the 'Save' Button
